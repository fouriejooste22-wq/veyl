Standing Water

Ferrow’s Crossing, Vaelmire Basin. A drowned mill where three rivers meet. Vantrell have a survey crew working the riverbed and guards on the approaches. Nobody on this team carries a weapon — you observe, you record, and you leave.

  • Photograph the three Vantrell survey markers
  • Recover the hydrologist’s field notebook from the mill office
  • Leave by the southern reed channel
  • Optional — locate the three missing dredge crew

Difficulty

Graphics

Shadow resolution, draw distance, vegetation density and rain. Drop it if the frame counter sits below 30.

This is a browser prototype, not the finished game. Real 3D with dynamic shadows, volumetric-style fog and rain — but WebGL on a tablet is not Unreal Engine 5 on a desktop GPU. No Nanite, no Lumen, no photogrammetry. It exists to test how the stealth loop plays in three dimensions.

The UE5 project on your laptop is still where the photorealistic target lives.

Controls

How to move

Touch

Left thumb on the circle to move. Drag anywhere else to swing the camera. Pinch to pull it in and out. Right-hand buttons: stance, use, your ability, acoustic emitter.

Keyboard and mouse

W A S D move   mouse drag look   Shift slow   C crouch   Z prone   E use   Q ability   F emitter   Esc menu

Being seen

Reeds, shadow and a low stance hide you. Lamp light, open ground and running give you away. A guard who notices you fills a meter above their head — break line of sight before it fills and they only come looking. If it fills, they call it in.

Nobody dies. A compromised operative is pinned until a teammate reaches them. If the site goes to full alert the team is extracted and the operation ends on a worse outcome.
