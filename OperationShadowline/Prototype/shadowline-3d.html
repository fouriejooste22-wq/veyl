Inventory

Hale Terraces, Kolda Range. Nine villages on the valley shoulders, emptied in a single night. Vantrell have a site warden counting what the people left behind, down to the livestock — and that inventory is the evidence. Come up the valley, work the terraces, leave over the north pass.

  • Photograph the three survey stakes above the terraces
  • Recover the village inventory from the warden’s field office
  • Leave over the north pass
  • Optional — find the three roadside shrines

The mountain

Slopes steeper than about fifty degrees cannot be climbed, so the valley floor, the terraces and the switchback road are the ways through. Snow above the treeline leaves you with nowhere to hide; the pines, the terrace walls and a low stance are your cover.

Nobody dies. A compromised operative is pinned until a teammate reaches them. Full alert means the team is pulled out and the operation ends on a worse outcome.
