Standing Water

Ferrow’s Crossing, Vaelmire Basin. A drowned mill where three rivers meet. Vantrell have a survey crew working the riverbed and guards on the approaches. Nobody on this team carries a weapon — you observe, you record, and you leave.

  • Photograph the three Vantrell survey markers
  • Recover the hydrologist’s field notebook from the mill office
  • Leave by the southern reed channel
  • Optional — locate the three missing dredge crew

Difficulty

This is a field prototype, not the game. It is a browser build made to test the stealth loop — detection, concealment, companion behaviour, the difficulty knobs — before any of it is built in Unreal Engine 5. The UE5 project is unchanged and still the real target.

Co-op is written but switched off. Up to four players, one position each, synchronised live. It needs a capability that opens a channel between everyone viewing the page, and that needs your say-so — so for now the other three positions are run by the companion AI.

Controls

How to move

Touch

Left thumb on the circle to move. Right-hand buttons: stance, use, your ability, acoustic emitter. Drag anywhere on the map to look around; pinch to zoom.

Keyboard

W A S D move   Shift slow walk   C crouch   Z prone   E use   Q ability   F emitter   Tab objectives

Being seen

Reeds, shadow and a low stance hide you. Light pools, open ground and sprinting give you away. A guard who notices you fills a meter above their head — break line of sight before it fills and they only come looking. If it fills, they call it in.

Nobody dies here. A compromised operative is pinned down until a teammate reaches them. If the whole site goes to full alert, the team is extracted and the operation ends on a worse outcome — not a failure screen.
